Milt's Golf Center

9-Hole Par 3 Course & Driving Range

Not everyone in Omaha has the time to enjoy their favorite pastime once a week, but all of us have a few minutes to spend some quality practice time hitting a bucket of golf balls. Swing on by Milt's Golf Course and Range for some fun golf when you get the chance and enjoy high quality range balls with your choice of real grass or artificial turf hitting surfaces. Practice makes perfect and we look forward to the opportunity to help make even a small contribution to your golf game improvement.

9-Hole Par 3

A friendly, walkable layout suited to golfers at all skill level, from first-timers to experienced golfers.

Driving Range

300 yard range with your choice of real grass or artificial turf hitting surfaces.

Practice Greens

A pitching and chipping green plus a putting green to work on your short game.

Lessons

Work on your swing or dial in your short game with expert coaching.
